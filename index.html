About

Hi, I'm Miguel, a Dallas-based creative director and designer with 10+ years of hands-on brand, web, and campaign work, recognized along the way with World Brand Design Society honors for packaging and branding. My background runs through freelance, in-house, and agency work, leading teams of designers across food and beverage, real estate, beauty, pet care, technology, and industrial brands.

I've spent the last few years expanding my craft from branding into consumer product campaigns, as well as exploring AI's capabilities and putting them to work inside the design process. Outside client work, I'm usually near a canvas, a half-finished build, or a live show. Bilingual, always building something, and happiest working with people who care about the details as much as I do.
